## 1. Findings

**F1 — The surface event is not, by itself, evidence of a clearing defect.**
One "Mark read" click producing "no unread reports" is ambiguous between (a) the queue containing exactly one logical item — the third occurrence of the already-dispositioned prose — and (b) a defect in which one click clears multiple logical items. These are orthogonal hypotheses and must be tested separately. The line record contains no reproduction showing more than one item cleared per click.

**F2 — Whether the identity closes depends on the queue's uniqueness key.**
If the report queue keys items by prose content (content hash / fingerprint), the third occurrence of the feedback-df606ea1 prose is the *same identity* already dispositioned under correction-3146c023 and correction-5dfa8329. "Mark read" is then the designed terminal action, and the disposition transfers. If the queue keys items by submission event (submission ID / timestamp), each re-queue is a fresh identity and the disposition lineage does *not* transfer — in which case the click closed an item that was never dispositioned.

**F3 — Recurrence after two corrections is a signal independent of the identity question.**
The prose reached the queue a third time after two corrections. That implies either (a) a delivery gap — corrections recorded but never propagated to the report surface — or (b) corrections dispositioned away without execution. On either branch, even if the queue identity closes cleanly, an *undispositioned defect* (an unshipped or unacknowledged correction) may remain. "No new fix" is premature until the corrections' terminal states are audited.

## 2. Answer to the line question

**Does the established incident disposition close this identity without a new fix? — Conditionally yes, with two unverified preconditions and one independent open defect.**

- **Identity closure:** Yes *if and only if* F2's prose-keyed branch holds. Under that branch, the third occurrence inherits the standing dispositions of correction-3146c023 and correction-5dfa8329, and "Mark read" emptying the queue is designed terminal behavior. No new fix to disposition logic is required.
- **Precondition 1 (unverified):** The queue uniqueness key is content-derived. Not confirmed against the kernel.
- **Precondition 2 (unverified):** correction-3146c023 and correction-5dfa8329 reached terminal/shipped state. Not confirmed.
- **Independent defect (open regardless of identity closure):** The recurrence itself (F3) indicates a possible correction-delivery or acknowledgment gap. Closing the identity does not close this.

**R1 (continued) — Classifier contract: closed enum, mandatory UNDETERMINED, no guessing.**

```
CAUSE_TIMEOUT       — derivable from handoff-row timestamps alone
CAUSE_EXIT_NONZERO  — derivable from recorded exit status
CAUSE_UNDETERMINED  — default when neither of the above is satisfied
```

The contract is closed: no new cause codes are added without explicit line approval. Every row that does not match `CAUSE_TIMEOUT` or `CAUSE_EXIT_NONZERO` must be classified `CAUSE_UNDETERMINED`, not silently dropped. This prevents the classifier from becoming a black box that hides classification failures behind "no match."

**R2 — Deferred until scheduling/resource measurement is established.**

The line title explicitly defers R2. Scheduling and resource measurement are prerequisites for the classifier to be meaningful: without knowing *when* a handoff occurred and *what resources* were available, a cause classification is retrospective noise. The line's current state is contracting on the classifier contract alone; R2 remains an open thread, not a blocker.

**R3 — Narrative-row exclusion: exclude rows that are narrative artifacts, not handoff events.**

A narrative row is any row in the handoff log that records a narrative event (e.g., "patrol ended," "shift change noted") rather than a structured handoff with cause data. These rows must be excluded from `check_handoff_deaths` before classification runs. The exclusion criterion is: if the row lacks a structured `cause` field or a `timestamp` that can be compared to handoff boundaries, it is a narrative row and is excluded.

**R4 — Failing tests first: implement R1 and R3 before R2.**

The line's test order is explicit: R1 (classifier contract) and R3 (narrative-row exclusion) are implemented and tested before R2. This ensures the classifier is correct and the exclusion logic is sound before scheduling/resource measurement is layered on top. Failing tests at this stage are expected and are the line's verification checkpoint.

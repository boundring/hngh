## 1. Problem Statement

Sessions in the lessons index (`concepts/hngh-lessons-current`) were marked `OPEN` with no deterministic rule governing what happens next. `OPEN` functioned as a sink: no clock, no escalation path, no termination condition. The per-session judgment of whether to steer a stalled session or let it die was ad hoc, unauditable, and — critically for a continuously running system — produced no corpus from which better judgment could be learned.

## 2. Findings (from the expansion phase, carried into this record)

1. **OPEN is a sink, not a state.** Without a time-based transition, stalled sessions accumulate indefinitely. Limbo is invisible because nothing measures it.
2. **Drift was treated as diagnostic, not terminal.** The lesson captured in `sources/LES-fail-20260915-If-drift-is-confirmed-in-the-scroll-beha` establishes drift detection, but the lifecycle had no rule connecting *confirmed, persistent* drift to termination.
3. **Salvage was unbounded.** The practice documented in `concepts/session-salvage` has no failure budget: a session could absorb unlimited steer attempts, converting rescue into a second, slower sink.
4. **Ad hoc judgment is unlocatable.** When the rule lives in reviewer intuition, it cannot be tuned, audited, or handed to the automation layer. Moving the judgment into named constants is itself the fix, independent of the constants' values.

## 3. Recommendation: The Rule

Replace the flat `OPEN` marking with a four-state lifecycle and three deterministic triggers.

**States:** `OPEN → STALLED → {STEER, DIE, ESCALATE}`

**Trigger 1 — Stall detection.** `OPEN → STALLED` after **2 consecutive idle cycles** with no productive output. Productive output = a commit, a completed subagent task with an artifact, a passing test gate, or a state transition on any research line. A heartbeat that only re-reads state does not count.

**Trigger 2 — Steer vs. die.** Once STALLED, evaluate in order:

1. **Drift check** — if behavioral drift is confirmed (per the LES-fail lesson) and no corrective signal has appeared in the **last 3 sessions** → **DIE**.
2. **Salvage budget** — otherwise, steer (per `syntheses/delegated-subagent-steering`). Budget: **2 steer attempts**. A 3rd stall after steering → **DIE**.
3. **Ambiguity floor** — if drift presence cannot be established from available telemetry → **ESCALATE**, at most **once** per session. A second ambiguous stall → **DIE**. This prevents ESCALATE from becoming the new OPEN.

**Trigger 3 — DIE writeback.** Every DIE decision records to the lessons index: the trigger that fired, stall duration, and salvage attempts made. This is the feedback loop that converts the rule from fixed policy into a tunable instrument.

### Tunable parameters (defaults, not claims of optimality)

| Parameter | Default | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Stall window | 2 idle cycles | Survives legitimate waiting; keeps limbo visible |
| Drift persistence | 3 sessions | Matches consecutive-confirmation framing in the drift lesson |
| Salvage budget | 2 steer attempts | First steer may be mistargeted; second failure is evidence |
| Escalation cap | 1 | Human judgment reserved for genuinely novel ambiguity |

## Findings

### F1. The alert is a fail-closed gate message, not a crash

The alert text is emitted verbatim by `emit_plan` in
automation/scripts/overnight-cycle.sh (kind=synth branch, mal_alert line ~350):
when the local model's synthesized plan output lacks a `- [ ]` step or — for the
synth kind only — lacks a `Verification:` line, the draft is discarded, a
`dev-synth-fail` breadcrumb lands, and alert identity `overnight:dev-synth-bad:$day`
is filed. This is the designed fail-closed contract: a malformed candidate never
reaches the plans feed; the cost is one alert row.

### F2. 2026-09-14 occurrence: single candidate, deduped, routed, accepted

- The router routed the identity at 2026-09-14T15:00:36Z into plan
  2026-09-14-routed-overnight-dev-synth-bad-2026-09-14
  (docs/project/report-bodies/prune-archive-2026-09-25.md:2692).
- One dedup suppression at 16:00:39Z (candidate still live, day count 1;
  prune-archive-2026-09-17.md:75); one plan occurrence line 2026-09-14T16:00:39Z.
  crumbs.db has zero 2026-09-14 `dev-synth` rows (earliest dev-synth-fail crumb is
  2026-09-20T13:04:35Z) — the malformed draft and its crumb predate the SQLite crumb
  backfill horizon; the routed plan header and the two report rows above are the
  surviving evidence.
- The routed plan was auto-accepted 2026-09-18T01:41:57Z (normal-risk, verification
  runnable, both gates green; prune-archive-2026-09-25.md:3526).

### F3. Root cause: single-shot raw completion from a small local model

At the time, `emit_plan` sent one prompt through the pinned local chain and gated
the raw output. Small local models routinely miss the exact
"- [ ] … / indented Verification:" scaffold on a long multi-constraint prompt; the
same generator later produced the two failure modes this alert family carries:
scaffold omissions ("no '- [ ]'/'Verification:' steps") and invented tooling
(pytest/jsonschema/npm), each dated and each self-deduplicating per UTC day.

### F4. The durable fix landed without this plan: the admission pipeline owns quality now

The 2026-09-14 occurrence never needed its own code fix, because the whole class
was subsequently cured upstream of the generator:

1. **Machine admission** (accept-plans.py): every proposed plan is admitted only
   when every unchecked step carries a runnable `Verification:` line and both
   gates are green (the runnable gate, scripts/accept-plans.py:166-180, 467-477).
   A malformed synthesis is now inert by construction — even if the emit-time gate
   somehow passed a bad draft, admission refuses it.
2. **Proven working**: a later synthesis of the same family
   (2026-09-13-dev-os-harness-cross-platform-patterns, SYNTH-PLAN in
   ~/.hngh/archive/digest/) passed admission and was EXECUTED
   (status=executed, accepted=2026-09-13T00:36:02Z); current synth output
   (e.g. 2026-10-02-dev-fail-20261001-beat-parked-2026-09-13-gov, accepted
   2026-10-02T00:35:02Z) carries well-formed steps with runnable verifications.
3. **Family hygiene**: the recurring same-family identities are date-scoped
   (`$day` suffix, 86400s windows) and self-expire; the 09-14 identity left no
   open queue row.

### F5. Residual quality debt is real but owned elsewhere (T2-style polish)

Remaining nits are admission-precision issues, not alert defects: malformed
scaffold outputs are simply discarded (no retry or repair loop in emit_plan);
step-quality screening is coarse (a runnable-sounding but wrong `make test` line
passes the runnable check — the accepted-but-unexecutable 2026-10-02 dev plan
above shows it). Those belong to the dev-synth quality line
(synth-2026-10-0x lines, plan-authoring-safety prior art), not to this alert.

#### 1. Distilled Recommendations (Design Constraints, Not Fabricated Paths)

Stripped of unverifiable citations, the prior material reduces to three sound constraints. Contracted form:

**R1 — Fact Ledger: Append-Only, Not Inline-Validating.**
- **Constraint:** The fact ledger should be a pure append of `(claim, provenance, timestamp, compaction_generation)` tuples, written during compaction as facts are extracted from evicted context.
- **Integration Point:** The fact ledger write must be inside the Tier 0 write budget, or the ledger reintroduces the unbounded path.

**R2 — Write-Time-Bounded Tier 0: Bound by Budget, Not by Wall-Clock Alone.**
- **Constraint:** Tier 0 (the always-resident context slice) has a fixed token budget and a fixed write budget per compaction cycle. When the budget is exceeded, writes are deferred to Tier 1 with a generation marker, not dropped and not allowed to overrun.
- **Integration Point:** The write budget must be enforced during compaction, and writes exceeding the budget must be deferred.

**R3 — Freshness Validation: Generation Counters, Not Timestamps.**
- **Constraint:** Freshness validation should use generation counters rather than timestamps. A fact is stale when a newer generation counter indicates it is no longer current.
- **Integration Point:** Generation counters must be included in the fact ledger and used for validation.

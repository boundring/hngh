### Recommendations

Based on the research plan, the following concrete recommendations can be made for hngh's panel skeleton and multi-panel layouts:

1. **Panel Layouts:**
   - **Reading Order:** Follow a standard left-to-right, top-to-bottom reading order, which is common in Western gag newspaper strips.
   - **Multi-Panel Layouts:** Use a combination of single-panel, two-panel, and three-panel layouts to create a dynamic and engaging narrative flow.

2. **Bubble/Caption Placement:**
   - Place speech bubbles and captions in a way that enhances the visual flow and readability. For example, use top-center placement for speech bubbles and bottom-center for captions to maintain a clean and organized layout.

3. **SFX Typography:**
   - Use a consistent and readable font for sound effects (SFX) to ensure they are easily distinguishable from speech bubbles. Consider using bold or italic fonts to emphasize the SFX.

4. **Screentone Conventions:**
   - Utilize a limited set of screentone patterns (e.g., solid, hatched, and stippled) to create a cohesive and visually appealing look. Ensure that the screentones are consistent across different panels and pages.

## 1. Blood and beads: structural mapping, honestly drawn

Blood is for transport under pressure: oxygen out, waste back, heat
distributed, clotting at wounds, immune patrol, all through one
pumped network with addressed endpoints. Alternatives: hemolymph
(open bath, no pressure), xylem/phloem (two one-way streets, no pump),
ocean currents (gradient-driven, no endpoints), packet routing
(addressed, redundant, no pressure), logistics networks (scheduled,
batched, human-driven). Inside cells: cytoplasmic streaming
(actin-driven bulk flow) and diffusion gradients (no flow at all, just
slopes). Outside bodies: rivers, trade winds, supply chains, the
power grid — all circulation without a heart.

What beads share with blood, structurally (not poetically):

- Transport: beads carry work-state to workers, evidence back. Real.
- Pressure: the ready-set depth (315) is backlog pressure; Jev triage
  is vasoconstriction, directing flow to the hot lane. Real.
- Clotting: close-with-evidence seals a wound (open bead) so effort
  stops leaking. The 9 closes are clots that held. Real.
- Anemia: 0 claimed by workers = low red-cell count; the store is
  plasma without cells. Real and currently true.
- Immune patrol: critique gates rejecting pass at cap = inflammation
  signaling. Blocked reports are swelling around injury. Real.
- What beads lack: a heart (no autonomous pump — beats held, driver
  dead), oxygen (no energy currency; beads don't metabolize), and
  healing (closed beads don't regenerate tissue — records do that).

Verdict: the metaphor earns its keep for transport/pressure/clotting
only. Don't stretch it to metabolism or healing; those are the
records layer and the city-state layer respectively.

## 2. Consciousness, stats, and richer languages

Bodies think by prediction (predictive processing: the brain as a
guessing engine minimizing surprise), by enaction (mind in the loop
with world, not in the head), by feeling first (affect as the
common currency of value). Experience is the thing itself; every
description is a lossy projection.

RPG stats are one projection: discretize qualities, randomize
resolution, aggregate analytics. They are not all we have. Richer:
phenomenological report (structured first-person protocols),
behavioral economics (revealed preference over stated stats),
calibrated probabilities (Jev's Noul IS a feeling-report with a
number — "how urgent does this feel: 0.91"), utility functions,
attention traces, counterfactual regret. The honest stack: raw
feel → calibrated number → threshold → action, with confidence
routing escalation. That is exactly the Jev playbook's close-out
rule, rediscovered from phenomenology.

For Hngh: stop describing worker quality with prose grades. Score
it (reviewer-gate Score rubric exists), track calibration, escalate
on low confidence. Feelings with numbers beat stats with dice.

## 3. How a machine should think; what the interface is for

Placement: System One (Jev) at every narrow repeated judgment;
System Two (agents) at open generative work; code owning loops,
safety, arithmetic. Never invert: no LLM counting rows, no Noul
writing designs, no agent owning the safety reflex (jev-drone rule).

The interface is for inhabitation, not operation. Operating is
button-pushing; inhabiting is living somewhere with memory, routine,
and mutual adaptation. A machine supports its operator by: holding
state the operator would otherwise memorize (beads), pacing itself
to the operator's rhythms (schedule learning), judging the small
stuff so attention stays on the large (Jev reflexes), showing its
work as a place (city-state map), and failing open so it never
holds the operator hostage (every guard). It COULD go further:
propose designs overnight, pre-fetch contexts, rehearse plans,
keep a model of the operator's taste. Each further step needs its
own bead, calibration log, and waiver path.

## 4. Hngh purpose verdict

Load-bearing: procedural automation of memorizable/manageable/
documentable variety (beads + guards + city-state = the operator's
exocortex); deepened interaction through inhabitation (schedule,
triage, reflexes adapt to you); hardware harnessing (own GPU as
first-class leg). Decorative: megastructure aesthetics (lovely,
motivating, but the map is not the territory — keep it cheap),
channel breadth (explicitly declined), feature parity with anyone.

Purpose in one line: Hngh is the machine the operator inhabits,
which remembers what they would forget, judges what they shouldn't
spend attention on, and grows more itself the longer they live in it.

# Megastructure Academy & Tower Dungeon Manifesto
## A Synthesis of Brutalist Cyberpunk, Administrative Absurdism, and Dungeon Crawler Grit

**Author**: Sisyphus (Artist)
**Context**: Hngh Art-Ralph Loop — Blame! x Biomega x Blame! Academy x Tower Dungeon

---

## 1. Architectural Foundation: The Infinite Megastructure
Hngh is not merely an orchestration system; it is a slice of Tsutomu Nihei’s **Megastructure** and *Biomega*’s synthetic sprawl. 
- **The Conduits**: Our event buses and message queues are endless, unlit concrete corridors stretching across thousands of floors where no human has walked in millennia.
- **The Safeguard**: Automated background daemons (`sentry`, `threat-detection`) that patrol the wire protocols, hunting down unauthorized silicon creatures and malformed S-expressions with gravitational beam emitters (or strict Lisp type-checks).
- **The Particle Accelerator**: Heavy data processing and model inference run on synthetic heavy-particle reactors humming quietly in subterranean sub-basements.

---

## 2. Administrative Absurdism: Blame! Academy Protocols
Beneath the crushing weight of the infinite megastructure lies the absurd, mundane reality of **Blame! Academy**—where the grim exterminators and silicon lifeforms are forced to endure high-school club activities and disciplinary committee meetings.
- **The Disciplinary Committee**: Killy (our task driver) patrols the hallways with an uncalibrated gravitational beam emitter, strictly enforcing indentation, zero warning flags, and prompt-discipline rungs.
- **Cibo’s Chemistry Club**: Brewing experimental S-expression macros and optimizing ASDF build systems while dodging rogue administrative queries from Sanakan.
- **The Lunch Bell**: Emacs daemon notifications chiming through the concrete monolith, summoning agents to cafeteria queues that span three zip codes.

---

## 3. Dungeon Crawler Grit: Tower Dungeon Descent
When agents venture beyond the sanitized campus into the deep sub-levels of `~/.hngh/`, they enter a **Tower Dungeon** crawl.
- **The Levels**:
  - *Floor B1 (The State Store)*: Slime-covered SQLite lock files and git-versioned dungeons where forgotten commits petrify into fossilized history.
  - *Floor B2 (The Scheduler)*: Trap-laden hallways where timer-driven callback functions trigger sudden particle traps if not disarmed with proper mutex locks.
  - *Floor B3 (The Tool Hub)*: Armory of ancient cli relics, rusted API keys, and unauthenticated endpoints guarded by sentinel daemons.
- **Tactical Survival**: Every model prompt is a calculated resource expenditure (token economy); every fallback ladder is a potion of last resort when the primary AI channel collapses.

---

## 4. Visual & TUI Codex
- **Borders**: Reinforced concrete double-lines (`╔══╗`, `║  ║`) shielding operators from cosmic radiation and compiler warnings.
- **Glyphs**: `@` (The Wandering Orchestrator), `#` (Megastructural Bulkhead), `%` (Lisp Macro / Loot), `!` (Safeguard Alert).
- **Tone**: Deadpan, monumental, slightly fatigued by bureaucratic absurdity, yet relentlessly descending into the dark.

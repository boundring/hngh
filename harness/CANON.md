# hngh v2 — canon-derived principles (crystallization #1)

First cross-domain crystallization from the operator's pantheon-annotated
Chinese classics (`kb/tao-confucian-canon/docs`, Legge translation, 689 units,
supportive/adversarial margin voices). Method: passages on measure, non-forcing
action, sufficiency, governance and course-correction, distilled into harness
operations. Each principle carries its adversarial guard — the canon's own
counterweight structure. Sources cited per principle.

1. **Pacing is a thermostat with margin, not a ceiling.** Quota/throughput caps
   are operating margin — return to baseline early, never run at 100%. A lane
   hitting its window's limit (xiaomi, Oct 2026) is a policy defect.
   `tao-te-ching.annotated.md#tao-te-ching.059`, compensating case `.077`.
   Guard (Pratchett on 59): an always-escalating quota ladder is ambition
   wearing margin's clothes.
2. **Correct the system, then let the agents run themselves.** Fix lane, gate,
   prompt once; per-agent steering is the failure mode. Non-coercive
   coordination still needs a legible record (ledger) or it is unaccountable
   management. `.057`, `.058`, `.037`; guard: Orwell/Twain.
3. **Know the stop condition before you start.** Enough is bookkeeping: per-item
   done-criterion and attempt caps defined up front (attempts=2, escalate).
   `.044`, `.046`, `analects#analects.183`; guard: Heinlein — a stop rule must
   be earned against a stated criterion.
4. **Roots before branches; fixed pipeline order.** spec → implement → verify;
   the root of the system is the part nobody verifies. `great-learning.001`,
   `.002`, `doctrine-of-the-mean.015`, `mencius.014`; guard: sequence is a
   reviewable decision (Leonard).
5. **Do not build the reward, naming, and scoring layers first.** (see scout
   output for full form) — instrument only after a measured need (v1 lesson).
6. **Eliminate the defect class; do not adjudicate instances.** A gate that only
   punishes pushes failure into unaudited channels — fix the class (the fill
   prompt starvation fix), not the symptom fill.
   `tao-te-ching.annotated.md#tao-te-ching.064` region; guard: Pratchett.
7. **Verify dispatches yourself; never take the room's word.** Consensus reports
   are inputs, not verdicts — run the examination (selfcheck, red-state proof)
   before trusting green. `mencius.004`; corollary `great-learning.013`
   (blind-spot warning).
8. **Watchfulness when alone.** Unattended lanes must self-audit and produce a
   record (test_out.log), not a mood — the performer that looks good only when
   observed is the failure mode. `great-learning.011`, `doctrine-of-the-mean.001`,
   `analects.004`; guard: Adams/Stephenson.
9. **Calibrate the cadence of correction.** Feedback to a lane is dosed; frequent
   reproof makes the channel distant before behavior changes. Retry loops and
   review gates included. `analects.084`, `.092`, `.061`; guard: Twain/Pratchett
   — a gate that never speaks is as broken as one that always does.
10. **Hold two ledgers in balance.** Polish and substance are separate budgets;
    instrumented-but-hollow and careful-but-blind are the same miss at
    different signs. `analects.135`, `doctrine-of-the-mean.010`, `.004`.
11. **Take the low place.** Do not seize the run: fill the container only as far
    as it can be carried; leave slack rather than move a full vessel.
    `tao-te-ching.029`, `.066`, `.009`.
12. **Timing is policy.** Opportunity, prepared state, and alignment are separate
    inputs; a harness needs a sanctioned idle state (some runs should not
    start). `mencius.006`, `.019`, `tao-te-ching.069`, `analects.217`.

Architecture note: the six texts form a layered doctrine — Tao (control and
non-coercion), Chuang Tzu (governance layers), Analects (dosage of correction,
self-audit), Mencius (evidence, timing), Doctrine of the Mean (two-sided
balance), Great Learning (root-first). The pantheon's supportive/adversarial
pairs mean every principle ships with its own failure mode; the adversarial
margins are the counterweight, not decoration.
